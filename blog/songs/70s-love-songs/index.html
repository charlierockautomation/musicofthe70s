The Short Answer: What Makes a 70s Love Song

A 70s love song is a Billboard Hot 100 hit from 1970 to 1979 whose subject is wanting, keeping or losing someone.

That covers more than slow dances.

It takes in tender soft rock, soul ballads, film themes and a few songs that argue the other side.

The decade's chart rewarded all of them, and 27 of the 30 on this page reached number one.

Here is what the list shows at a glance:

  • The earliest song on the list charted in 1970, and the latest reached number one in 1979.
  • 1973 has the most songs on the list, with five.
  • The longest run at number one belongs to one song: ten straight weeks.
  • Three songs missed number one, and each has a story of its own further down.

70s Love Songs Were Not Always Soft

The list holds quiet piano ballads, but it also holds a lover's plea over a rhythm section.

"Let's Get It On" and "Kiss and Say Goodbye" are both love songs in the decade's terms.

One asks for closeness and the other says a hard goodbye.

So the label here follows the lyric's subject, not the tempo.

How These 70s Love Songs Were Chosen

The starting pool came from this site's own song data, not from a critic's mood board.

It holds 1,000 year-end Hot 100 songs for 1970 to 1979, and each one carries a mood tag.

227 of them are tagged romantic and have a YouTube video on record.

From that pool, 30 made the list on three tests:

  • The song is a love song in its lyric, not just in its title.
  • Its chart facts can be checked against the weekly or year-end Billboard data on this site.
  • Its story line can be backed by this site's own Songs pages or by a source named at the bottom of the page.

That is why the list leans toward big chart hits.

A song with a great reputation but no checkable chart record did not make the cut.

Ten Weeks for Debby Boone

"You Light Up My Life" went to number one in October 1977 and stayed for ten straight weeks.

That is the longest run of the whole decade, not just among love songs.

It ranked third on the 1978 year-end list.

It is also on this site's 70s one hit wonders list, since it was Boone's only Top 40 hit.

Roberta Flack Owns Two Slots

Roberta Flack appears twice in the top five longest runs.

"The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face" held six weeks in 1972, the longest unbroken run of that year.

"Killing Me Softly with His Song" followed with five non-consecutive weeks in 1973.

That five-week total made it the longest-running number one of its year.

First Number Ones: Acts That Broke Through With a Love Song

A love song was often the one that finally put an act on top.

These acts reached number one for the first time with a song on this list:

  • The Carpenters, "(They Long to Be) Close to You," 1970.
  • Al Green, "Let's Stay Together," 1972.
  • Gladys Knight & the Pips, "Midnight Train to Georgia," 1973.
  • Olivia Newton-John, "I Honestly Love You," 1974.
  • Barry Manilow, "Mandy," 1975.
  • The Eagles, "Best of My Love," 1975.
  • The Manhattans, "Kiss and Say Goodbye," 1976.
  • Chicago, "If You Leave Me Now," 1976.
  • Andy Gibb, "I Just Want to Be Your Everything," 1977.
  • The Commodores, "Three Times a Lady," 1978.
  • Peaches & Herb, "Reunited," 1979.

For several of these acts, the love song became the song they were known for.

The Carpenters, Al Green and the Eagles all have fuller pages here, including Carpenters songs.

Bread and Minnie Riperton Hit the Top Early

Bread reached number one with its first Hot 100 entry, "Make It with You," in August 1970.

Minnie Riperton took "Lovin' You" to number one in April 1975.

It remained the only Top 40 hit of her career.

Love Songs That Stopped Short of Number One

Three songs on the list never topped the Hot 100.

Each one is here because the chart does not tell the whole story.

"We've Only Just Begun" Started as a Bank Ad

Paul Williams and Roger Nichols wrote it in a single afternoon, for a bank commercial.

Richard Carpenter heard it on TV and asked for more.

The Carpenters released it on August 21, 1970, and it stopped at number two.

The full story is on the We've Only Just Begun page.

"Tired of Being Alone" Took Five Tries

Al Green wrote it in about half an hour, the morning after a show in Detroit.

Four other singles from the same album came out first, and none cracked the pop chart's upper half.

When this one reached radio in June 1971, it climbed to number 11.

It also reached number four in Britain.

"Hopelessly Devoted to You" Lost the Oscar

John Farrar wrote it for Sandy in Grease, partway through the shoot.

It peaked at number three on the Hot 100.

It also lost the Oscar.

The Hopelessly Devoted to You page has the rest.

1973 Is the Peak Year

Five songs from 1973 made the list, and every one of them reached number one.

That year gave us "Killing Me Softly with His Song," "Let's Get It On," "Touch Me in the Morning," "You Are the Sunshine of My Life" and "Midnight Train to Georgia."

It was also the year of Stevie Wonder's second number one of the year, after "Superstition."

The Decade Ends on the Bee Gees

"Too Much Heaven" knocked "Le Freak" off the top twice in January 1979.

The Bee Gees gave all of the proceeds to UNICEF.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best 70s love songs?

The best 70s love songs on this site's list include The Carpenters' "(They Long to Be) Close to You," Roberta Flack's "The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face," Marvin Gaye's "Let's Get It On," and Chicago's "If You Leave Me Now." All 30 are verified against Billboard chart data and can be played from the table at the top of the page.

What is the number one love song of the 70s?

Debby Boone's "You Light Up My Life" had the longest run at number one of any love song on this list. It spent ten straight weeks on top of the Billboard Hot 100 starting in October 1977, the longest run of the decade. Roberta Flack's "The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face" is next, with six weeks.

Which 70s love songs were written for someone else?

Several 70s love songs began with another singer or purpose. "How Deep Is Your Love" was written with Yvonne Elliman in mind, not the Bee Gees themselves. "Midnight Train to Georgia" began life as "Midnight Plane to Houston," and "We've Only Just Begun" started as music for a bank commercial before the Carpenters recorded it.

Did every 70s love song on this list reach number one?

No, 27 of the 30 love songs on this list reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100. The Carpenters' "We've Only Just Begun" stopped at number two, Olivia Newton-John's "Hopelessly Devoted to You" peaked at number three, and Al Green's "Tired of Being Alone" reached number 11.
