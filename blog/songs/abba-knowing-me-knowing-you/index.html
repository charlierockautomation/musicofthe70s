ABBA Knowing Me Knowing You: A #1 in Five Countries

ABBA Knowing Me Knowing You reached number one in five different countries in 1977.

In the United States, it stalled at number 14 on the Hot 100.

Before it had that title at all, the song went by two different working names during production.

Benny Andersson and Bjorn Ulvaeus wrote it as the third single pulled from the album Arrival.

Anni-Frid Lyngstad carried the lead vocal on a song mostly remembered today for foreshadowing the band's own divorces.

ABBA Knowing Me Knowing You Started With Two Different Working Titles

Before ABBA Knowing Me Knowing You had its real title, the song went through two working names during production.

The first working title was "Ring It In."

That was replaced during the sessions with "Number One, Number One."

Stig Anderson, the band's manager and a credited co-writer, came up with the phrase "Knowing Me, Knowing You" to replace it.

Bjorn Ulvaeus then built the actual lyrics around that title.

He has said he pictured a house being emptied, with boxes standing against bare walls, to capture a relationship falling apart.

Two Recording Sessions, Two Months Apart

ABBA recorded the basic track on March 23, 1976, at Metronome Studios in Stockholm.

Benny Andersson played electric piano on that same session.

Guitarist Lasse Wellander did not add his solo lines until two months later, on May 24, 1976.

The finished single came out on February 18, 1977, as the third release pulled from the album Arrival.

"Dancing Queen" and "Money, Money, Money" had already gone out as Arrival's first two singles.

Arrival itself had been released the previous October, already a commercial hit across Europe.

A Global #1 That Barely Cracked the US Top 15

Knowing Me, Knowing You topped the charts in five different countries.

The United Kingdom, Ireland, West Germany, Mexico, and South Africa all sent it to number one.

In the United States, it peaked at only number 14 on the Billboard Hot 100.

This site's own year-end Billboard data confirms that same number 14 peak for 1977.

The song also reached the Top 10 in additional markets, including Australia and Canada.

Why the US Chart Never Caught Up

ABBA's US chart run kept building across the second half of the decade regardless of this single's placement.

"Dancing Queen" had already given the group its only US number one earlier that same year.

"Take a Chance on Me" would climb to number 3 the following year, keeping the group's American run intact.

Frida's Vocal and the Session Players Behind ABBA Knowing Me Knowing You

Anni-Frid Lyngstad, known within the band as Frida, delivered the song's lead vocal.

Agnetha Faltskog sang the supporting harmony parts underneath her.

The Session Musicians on the Track

A small group of session musicians filled out the rest of the March 1976 recording.

  • Benny Andersson: electric piano
  • Janne Schaffer: guitar
  • Rutger Gunnarsson: bass
  • Ola Brunkert: drums
  • Malando Gassama: percussion

Wellander's guitar solo was the one piece still missing when that March session wrapped.

He filled in those lines two months later, finishing the recording that would become ABBA Knowing Me Knowing You.

That official video, directed by Lasse Hallstrom, shows the group facing each other against changing colored backdrops.

Knowing Me, Knowing You's Long Afterlife

The song outlived its own 1977 chart run by decades.

It later became part of the stage musical Mamma Mia! and its film adaptations.

A completely different production borrowed its title too.

A British Comedy Borrowed the Title, Not the Song's Sadness

The BBC's "Knowing Me, Knowing You with Alan Partridge," Steve Coogan's comedy series, took its name directly from this ABBA song.

A performance of the track served as that show's actual theme music.

The fictional host's love of ABBA was the reason its producers picked it.

That connection makes the song a rare case of a decade-old chart hit inspiring an entirely different show two decades later.
