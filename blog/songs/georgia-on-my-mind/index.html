Georgia on My Mind: Willie Nelson's Surprise No. 1

Georgia on My Mind was already a standard by the time Willie Nelson touched it in 1977.

Ray Charles had owned the song for nearly two decades.

Nelson recorded it anyway, on an album his own label tried to talk him out of making.

It became his third solo number one.

It also won a Grammy and anchored an album that stayed on the charts for ten years.

This is the studio story, the chart record, and the strange coincidence tying his version back to Charles's.

Georgia on My Mind Came From an Album Columbia Didn't Want

By 1977 Willie Nelson had already proven the outlaw sound could sell.

Red Headed Stranger had gone platinum, and Waylon & Willie was still climbing the charts.

He wanted to make something else entirely next.

Nelson pitched Columbia an album of pop standards.

These were songs he had grown up singing on the radio, not one of them written in the outlaw era.

Label executives pushed back hard.

Nelson later recalled their reaction plainly: "This is not a good idea."

They told him it cost too much money and that nobody wanted to hear old songs like these anymore.

He made the record anyway.

Ten Songs, All Older Than His Outlaw Reputation

The album, titled Stardust, became his 22nd studio release.

Every one of its ten tracks was a pop standard he had chosen himself.

Georgia on My Mind sat among songs like "Blue Skies" and "All of Me."

None of them were country records in any conventional sense.

See how Willie Nelson built the rest of his 70s catalog around risks just like this one.

A Recording Truck Parked at Emmylou Harris's House

The Stardust sessions didn't happen inside a normal studio at all.

Nelson's neighbor in Malibu was musician Booker T. Jones, best known for leading Booker T. & the M.G.'s.

Nelson first asked Jones only to arrange one song, "Moonlight in Vermont."

Jones's work impressed him enough that Nelson handed him the whole album to produce.

A Mobile Studio in a Living Room

Jones booked the Enactron, a 42-foot mobile recording truck owned by Brian Ahern, Emmylou Harris's husband at the time.

The crew parked it in the driveway of Ahern and Harris's Beverly Hills home.

The sessions ran from December 3 to 12, 1977.

Musicians set up basic tracks right in the living room and back bedrooms.

Nelson's sister Bobbie played piano just a few feet from where he sang.

Harmonica player Mickey Raphael recorded his parts from inside the house's tiled bathroom shower.

Engineers had wired the shower for sound because of how the tile made the notes ring.

Ten days later, the whole record was finished.

A Country No. 1 the Same Year Georgia Adopted the Song

Georgia on My Mind became the song that proved Columbia wrong.

It came out as the album's first single on March 2, 1978.

This site's own Billboard country chart data confirms it reached number one for the week of June 10, 1978.

It was Nelson's third solo chart-topper as a performer.

That came just months after "Mammas Don't Let Your Babies Grow Up to Be Cowboys" and years after "Blue Eyes Crying in the Rain."

The Same Song, Two Very Different Legacies

Hoagy Carmichael and Stuart Gorrell wrote Georgia on My Mind back in 1930.

That was decades before either Nelson or Ray Charles touched it.

Ray Charles cut the definitive version in 1960.

It went to number one and became a signature song for the rest of his career.

Georgia's state legislature made Charles's recording the official state song on April 24, 1979.

That happened the same year Nelson picked up his own Grammy for the same song.

Frequently Asked Questions

Did Willie Nelson write Georgia on My Mind?

No, Hoagy Carmichael and Stuart Gorrell wrote Georgia on My Mind in 1930.

Willie Nelson recorded it as a cover for his 1978 album Stardust.

He picked it himself from a list of pop standards he had loved for years.

It was one of ten songs on the record.

All of them were older than the outlaw country sound he was known for at the time.

Did Willie Nelson's Georgia on My Mind reach number one?

Yes, this site's own Billboard country chart data confirms it.

It topped the Billboard Hot Country Singles chart for the week of June 10, 1978.

It was Nelson's third solo number one as a performer.

He also won the 1979 Grammy Award for Best Male Country Vocal Performance for the recording.

Why did Willie Nelson record an album of pop standards in 1978?

Nelson wanted to record the old songs he had grown up loving, not just outlaw country material.

His neighbor in Malibu, musician Booker T. Jones, helped arrange one track.

That impressed Nelson enough to hand him the whole album.

Columbia Records executives reportedly called it a bad idea.

They said old songs like these would not sell.

How is Willie Nelson's version connected to Ray Charles?

Ray Charles made Georgia on My Mind famous first, taking it to number one in 1960.

Georgia's state legislature adopted Charles's recording as the official state song on April 24, 1979.

That was the same year Nelson won his Grammy for his own recording.

Nelson and Charles later performed the song together on television specials.

How long did Stardust stay on the charts?

Stardust spent ten years on Billboard's Country Albums chart, logging that milestone on August 13, 1988.

It was the first album by any artist to reach ten years on that chart.

It also went platinum by December 1978, only eight months after release, the first of five platinum certifications.
