September Earth Wind and Fire: The No. 8 Hit That Stayed

By Charlie · Published October 18, 2026

▶ Jump to the video

September Earth Wind and Fire never got higher than number eight on the Billboard Hot 100.

It still became the band's signature song, and a date on the calendar.

Maurice White and Allee Willis wrote it in 1978, and it came out that November.

Below are the writing, the sound, the chart run, the certifications and the covers that followed.

September Earth Wind and Fire: Written by Allee Willis and Maurice White

A Hit Built for a Greatest Hits Album

September came out on November 18, 1978, on ARC/Columbia Records.

It was one of two new tracks on The Best of Earth, Wind & Fire, Vol. 1.

Wikipedia says the song was included on that compilation solely to boost sales with original content.

Allee Willis and Maurice White are credited as writers, and Al McKay is credited for the chord progression.

White also produced the record, which was recorded in September 1978.

The 21st Night

The lyric refers to the 21st night of September.

White said he chose 21 for how it sounded.

His wife Marilyn said September 21 was the expected birth date of their son Kahbran.

She described the song as a secret message between her, White and their son.

Wikipedia says White wrote the song over one month.

The Sound: A Four-Measure Groove and a Nonsense Hook

A Groove That Never Changes

Wikipedia describes the funk groove as a four-measure pattern built on a circle of fifths.

The pattern stays the same across the verses and choruses.

That is why the record feels like one long, easy swing.

Ba-Dee-Ya

Allee Willis was bothered by the nonsense words in the hook and asked White to rewrite them.

She later said she learned from him to never let the lyric get in the way of the groove.

The words stayed, and they are the part of the song everyone sings.

Who Played on It

Wikipedia lists the original musicians:

  • Maurice White and Philip Bailey on lead and background vocals, with Bailey also on congas.
  • Al McKay and Johnny Graham on guitar.
  • Verdine White on bass, with Fred White and Ralph Johnson on drums and percussion.
  • Larry Dunn on Rhodes piano and piano, and Andrew Woolfolk on soprano saxophone.
  • Rahmlee Michael Davis and Michael Harris on trumpet, Louis Satterfield on trombone and Paulinho da Costa on percussion.
  • Tom "Tom Tom 84" Washington on string, background vocal and horn arrangements.

Record World called it a smooth, quick song about autumn nostalgia in 1978, according to Wikipedia.

Philip Bailey considers it one of the group's best.

September Earth Wind and Fire on the Charts: Why It Peaked at No. 8

The Honest Number

September Earth Wind and Fire peaked at number eight on the Billboard Hot 100, according to Wikipedia.

It never reached number one there, and it does not appear among this site's weekly Hot 100 number ones.

On the US Hot Soul Singles chart it did reach number one.

In the UK it climbed to number three.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who wrote September by Earth Wind and Fire?

Allee Willis and Maurice White wrote September, according to Wikipedia.

Al McKay is credited with the chord progression and music sequence, and White produced the record.

It was recorded in September 1978 and released as a single on November 18, 1978.

What was September's peak on the Billboard Hot 100?

September peaked at number eight on the Billboard Hot 100, according to Wikipedia.

It reached number one on the US Hot Soul Singles chart and number three on the UK Singles chart.

This site's year-end data ranks it number 78 for 1979.

What does the 21st night of September mean in the song?

Maurice White chose the date for how it sounded, according to Wikipedia.

His wife Marilyn said September 21 was the expected birth date of their son Kahbran, and she described the song as a secret message between them and their son.

What does ba-dee-ya mean in September?

Nothing in particular.

Wikipedia says Allee Willis was bothered by the nonsense lyric and asked White to rewrite it.

She said she learned from him to never let the lyric get in the way of the groove, and the phrase stayed.

Is September by Earth Wind and Fire in the National Recording Registry?

Yes, Wikipedia says September was added to the Library of Congress National Recording Registry in 2018, which recognizes recordings of lasting cultural importance.

Rolling Stone also ranked it number 65 on its 2021 list of the 500 Greatest Songs of All Time.
