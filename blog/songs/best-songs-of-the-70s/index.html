The Top Song of Every Year

Each year-end No. 1 is the single that piled up the most chart points that year.

  • 1970: "Bridge Over Troubled Water" by Simon & Garfunkel
  • 1971: "Joy to the World" by Three Dog Night
  • 1972: "The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face" by Roberta Flack
  • 1973: "Tie a Yellow Ribbon Round the Ole Oak Tree" by Tony Orlando and Dawn
  • 1974: "The Way We Were" by Barbra Streisand
  • 1975: "Love Will Keep Us Together" by Captain & Tennille
  • 1976: "Silly Love Songs" by Wings
  • 1977: "Tonight's the Night (Gonna Be Alright)" by Rod Stewart
  • 1978: "Shadow Dancing" by Andy Gibb
  • 1979: "My Sharona" by The Knack

Ballads won the early years, and disco and new wave took the last two.

Records in the Weekly Chart Data

"You Light Up My Life" held the top spot for 10 straight weeks, from mid-October to late December 1977.

No other 70s single stayed at No. 1 that long.

The next longest runs were eight weeks each.

  • "Tonight's the Night (Gonna Be Alright)" by Rod Stewart
  • "Night Fever" by the Bee Gees

Andy Gibb's "Shadow Dancing" followed with seven weeks.

The Bee Gees and Their Nine No. 1s

The Bee Gees topped the weekly chart with nine different songs between 1971 and 1979.

  • "How Can You Mend a Broken Heart"
  • "Jive Talkin'"

  • "How Deep Is Your Love"
  • "Stayin' Alive"
  • "Night Fever"

Frequently Asked Questions

Who wrote "You Light Up My Life"?

Joseph Brooks wrote it for the film You Light Up My Life.

The song won him the Academy Award for Best Original Song at the 50th Academy Awards in 1978.

Debby Boone recorded the hit version in April 1977, and it was released on August 16, 1977.

What Grammy did Debby Boone win?

Debby Boone won Best New Artist at the 20th Annual Grammy Awards.

The other nominees were Foreigner, Andy Gibb, Stephen Bishop and Shaun Cassidy.

Gibb went on to finish first on the 1978 year-end chart, so she beat that year's top act to the award.

Where did "You Light Up My Life" finish on the year-end chart?

It placed No. 3 on the 1978 Year-End Hot 100, behind "Shadow Dancing" by Andy Gibb and "Night Fever" by the Bee Gees.

Its weeks at the top fell in late 1977, yet the 1977 year-end list leaves it out of its 100 places.

That is why it sits in the 1978 group of this ranking.

How many different songs reached No. 1 on the Hot 100 in the 70s?

253 different songs topped the weekly Hot 100 from 1970 to 1979, counted from this site's weekly chart data.

1974 was the busiest year, with 36 different songs holding No. 1 at some point.

1971 was the slowest, with 19.

Why do some songs in the list have no Play button?

This site only plays uploads from the artist's own channel or the record label.

For 30 of the 100 hits, no such upload of the original recording exists on YouTube yet, so those rows stay in the ranking without a player.
