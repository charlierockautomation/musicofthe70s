The Short Answer: What Makes a 70s Slow Song

A 70s slow song is a Hot 100 hit from 1970 to 1979 that sits at ballad pace and is tagged chill or reflect in this site's song data.

The song data holds no tempo field, so "slow" is an editorial call, not a beats-per-minute count.

Here is what the list shows at a glance:

  • 18 of the 30 songs carry the soft rock tag in this site's song data.
  • 21 of the 30 lead with a melancholy mood tag, so the decade's slow songs lean sad.
  • The longest runs at number one are six weeks, held by "Bridge Over Troubled Water" and "Alone Again (Naturally)."
  • 11 songs reached number one, and 19 stayed on the year-end lists only.

How These 70s Slow Songs Were Chosen

The starting pool came from this site's own song data.

It tags every year-end Hot 100 song for 1970 to 1979 with a vibe.

536 songs are tagged chill or reflect and have a YouTube video on record.

From that pool, 30 made the list on four tests:

  • Each year of the decade gets exactly three songs.
  • The song is not already on the 70s party, dance, love or road trip songs lists.
  • Its chart facts can be checked against the weekly or year-end Billboard data on this site.
  • Its chart line is computed from that data, never typed from memory.

32 songs in the slow pool already sit on those four companion lists, so they are left out here.

Where the Year-End Rank on These 70s Slow Songs Comes From

The Year-End Rank column is a position on that year's Billboard year-end list.

It is not the song's weekly peak.

1970 to 1972: Slow Songs Ruled the Top

Five of the eleven number ones came in these three years.

"Bridge Over Troubled Water" opened the decade with six weeks from February 28, 1970.

The Simon & Garfunkel story is on the Bridge Over Troubled Water page.

In 1972 "Without You" held four weeks from February 19.

"Heart of Gold" took over for one week on March 18, so two slow songs ran back to back.

1973 to 1975: Croce, Streisand and a Trio of Tears

"Time in a Bottle" reached number one on December 29, 1973, for two weeks.

It followed "The Most Beautiful Girl" and gave way to "The Joker."

"The Way We Were" is the third number one, first reaching the top on February 2, 1974.

1976 and 1977: A Quiet Middle

Six songs sit in these two years, and two reached number one.

Diana Ross's "Theme from Mahogany" took the top spot on January 24, 1976.

Mary MacGregor's "Torn Between Two Lovers" followed on February 5, 1977, for two weeks.

1978 and 1979: Neighbours on the Year-End List

Only one of the six songs here reached number one, Robert John's "Sad Eyes" on October 6, 1979.

Roberta Flack and Donny Hathaway's "The Closer I Get to You" is number 38 for 1978.

Kansas's "Dust in the Wind" is number 39 on the same year-end list, right beside it.

A Rank Is Not a Peak

The year-end list measures a whole year of chart life, not the highest weekly position.

So these ranks say how big the song was, not whether it climbed to the top.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best 70s slow songs?

The best 70s slow songs on this list include Simon & Garfunkel's "Bridge Over Troubled Water," Harry Nilsson's "Without You," Jim Croce's "Time in a Bottle" and Kansas's "Dust in the Wind." All 30 are checked against Billboard chart data, and every one can be played from the table at the top of the page, one after another.

What is the number one slow song of the 70s?

Simon & Garfunkel's "Bridge Over Troubled Water" and Gilbert O'Sullivan's "Alone Again (Naturally)" share the longest runs at number one on this list, six weeks each. 11 of the 30 slow songs reached number one on the weekly Billboard Hot 100 file on this site.

How do you decide what counts as a slow song?

A slow song here is a ballad picked from the songs this site tags chill or reflect. The song data holds no tempo (BPM) field, so the choice is an editorial judgment, not a measurement. Chart facts beside each song, such as year-end rank and weeks at number one, are checked against Billboard data.

Are 70s slow songs the same as 70s love songs?

No, slow songs and love songs are different lists. This list of 70s slow songs leans sad, with 21 of the 30 songs carrying melancholy as their first mood tag and only 5 carrying romantic. None of the 30 also appears on this site's 70s love songs list.

Where can I listen to 70s slow songs?

You can play all 30 songs from the table at the top of this page, with no sign-in. Press Play on any row and the list continues song after song until you press Stop. You can also open the same songs on Listen Now, this site's 70s music player.
