That's the way I like it, and in late 1975 a lot of America agreed.

Harry Wayne Casey and Richard Finch wrote the song in Florida for KC and the Sunshine Band.

That's the Way I Like It: Casey, Finch and a Toned-Down Single

Two Writers at TK Records

Harry Wayne Casey and Richard Finch wrote and produced the song, according to Wikipedia.

TK Records released it on June 10, 1975.

It was the second single from the band's self-titled second album.

Casey is the KC of the name.

You can read more about the band in our list of KC and the Sunshine Band songs.

Why the Single Was Toned Down

Wikipedia says the song was toned down from the original recording for the single and for radio airplay.

It also notes that the sexual overtones may have helped the song at discos.

That tension between a radio-safe edit and a club-ready groove is part of why it still sounds fresh.

Record World praised its "spirited, uplifting sound."

On the Charts: Number One Twice in One Month

Up, Down and Back to the Top

This site's weekly Hot 100 data lists the song at number one for the chart weeks of November 22 and December 20, 1975.

Wikipedia describes it as the band's second Hot 100 number one.

It topped the chart for one week and was replaced by Silver Convention's "Fly, Robin, Fly."

After that song spent three weeks at the top, KC and the Sunshine Band returned for one more week.

The song also spent one week at number one on the soul singles chart.

You can find it among the 70s number one hits.

Covers and Later Life of the Song

Three Covers

Wikipedia lists three notable covers, each with its own approach:

  • Dead or Alive, 1984: a single from the album Sophisticated Boom Boom that reached number 22 on the UK Singles Chart and became the group's first UK top 40 single.
  • Spin Doctors and Biz Markie, 1996: a version with Chris Barron singing the verses and Biz Markie rapping, released on the Space Jam soundtrack.
  • Clock, 1997: a Eurodance take that reached number 11 on the UK Singles Chart in 1998.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who wrote That's the Way I Like It?

Harry Wayne Casey and Richard Finch wrote That's the Way (I Like It), according to Wikipedia.

The pair also produced the record for KC and the Sunshine Band on TK Records.

It came out on June 10, 1975, as a single from the band's self-titled second album.

When did That's the Way I Like It reach number one?

That's the Way (I Like It) reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 twice in late 1975, according to Wikipedia.

This site's weekly Hot 100 data lists the chart weeks of November 22 and December 20, 1975.

Silver Convention's Fly, Robin, Fly held the top spot for three weeks in between.

How many weeks was That's the Way I Like It at number one?

That's the Way (I Like It) spent two weeks at number one, one week at a time.

Wikipedia says it topped the chart for one week, fell to Fly, Robin, Fly, then returned.

It also spent one week at number one on the soul singles chart.

Who covered That's the Way I Like It?

Dead or Alive, Spin Doctors with Biz Markie, and Clock covered That's the Way (I Like It).

Dead or Alive reached number 22 on the UK Singles Chart in 1984.

Clock took a Eurodance version to number 11 in the UK in 1998.

Is That's the Way I Like It a disco song?

Yes, Wikipedia lists That's the Way (I Like It) as disco and funk, according to its genre field.

It runs 3:06 as the radio edit and 5:07 on the album.

It came out on TK Records in 1975, and Casey and Finch produced it.

The single was toned down from the original recording for radio play.
