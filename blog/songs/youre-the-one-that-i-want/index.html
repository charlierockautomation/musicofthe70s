You're the One That I Want: Grease's Biggest Hit

By Charlie · Published October 21, 2026

▶ Jump to the video

You're the One That I Want is the duet John Travolta and Olivia Newton-John sing in the 1978 film Grease.

John Farrar wrote it for the movie, and the stage show never had it.

It spent nine weeks at number one in the UK and a week on top in the US.

This page covers the writing, the recording, the charts, the reception and the covers.

You're the One That I Want: Written for the Film

Farrar wrote the song for Newton-John's part in the film.

It was one of two songs he wrote for her, and the other was Hopelessly Devoted to You.

A Song the Stage Show Never Had

The song was not in the original stage musical.

The stage show had a similar number called All Choked Up, which had replaced an earlier song, Kiss It.

All Choked Up was written as a pastiche of Elvis Presley's All Shook Up, with a more provocative Sandy.

It was one of two songs by Jim Jacobs and Warren Casey cut entirely from both the film and its soundtrack.

Most 21st-century stage productions now use this song in its place.

The Director Was Not a Fan

Director Randal Kleiser disliked it.

He felt it did not fit the Casey and Jacobs score, or the 1950s style, in music or in words.

The film kept it anyway.

For more of the decade's pop, start at Music of the 70s and follow the pop tags.

The Showdown on Screen

Danny Zuko, played by Travolta, has lettered in cross-country running to win back Sandy Olsson.

Sandy, played by Newton-John, has turned into a greaser queen to win Danny back.

In the song, Danny reacts with shock and desire at her new look.

Sandy tells him he has to improve himself if he wants to treat her properly.

It is a flirty duet with a bubblegum pop hook.

Recording and Release

The song was recorded in 1977 and 1978 at Heider Studios and United/Western Studio.

David J. Holman recorded and mixed it, and Farrar produced.

Wikipedia lists the genre as pop and bubblegum pop.

On the Charts: Number One Around the World

The single reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100.

This site's weekly Hot 100 data puts it on top in the week of June 10, 1978.

It held the spot for one week.

Andy Gibb's Shadow Dancing took over the next week and stayed for seven.

Gold and Platinum in the US

The single was certified Gold by April 12, 1978.

The Platinum certification followed on July 18, 1978, for shipments of over 2 million.

Sales and Certifications

The single has sold over 4 million copies in the US and UK alone.

Estimates run to more than 15 million worldwide, which makes it one of the best-selling singles ever.

In the UK it sold 2 million copies and, as of 2018, ranked fifth among the best-selling singles of all time.

Reception, Rankings and Screen Afterlife

Record World called it a frantic, up-tempo duet between the two stars that was bound to leave listeners breathless.

AllMusic rated it favorable.

Later Rankings

  • Billboard included it in its 2023 staff list of the 500 best pop songs.
  • The Daily Telegraph included it in a 2017 list of the 50 best pop songs written for movies.

Later Versions and Covers

The film's twentieth anniversary brought a remix.

The 1998 Martian remix, issued by PolyGram, reached No. 4 in the UK and No. 27 in Australia.

The 1993 London Cast Version

Craig McLachlan and Debbie Gibson released their own version in 1993 on Epic Records.

It was the lead single from the Grease London revival cast recording, in which both starred.

It peaked at No. 13 on the UK Singles Chart.

Covers and Parodies

Frequently Asked Questions

Who wrote You're the One That I Want?

John Farrar wrote and produced You're the One That I Want.

He wrote it specifically for Olivia Newton-John's appearance in the 1978 film Grease.

It was not part of the original stage musical.

Farrar also wrote Hopelessly Devoted to You for her, and the two songs are the pair he made for the movie.

Did You're the One That I Want reach number one?

You're the One That I Want reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100, and this site's weekly data shows one week on top, the week of June 10, 1978.

In the UK it spent nine weeks at number one.

It also topped the charts in Australia, West Germany, Switzerland and several other countries.

Who sings You're the One That I Want in Grease?

John Travolta and Olivia Newton-John sing it as Danny Zuko and Sandy Olsson.

Danny has lettered in cross-country running to win Sandy back, and she has turned into a greaser queen.

RSO Records released the song as the second single from the Grease soundtrack, with Alone at a Drive-In Movie on the B-side.

Was You're the One That I Want in the original Grease musical?

You're the One That I Want was not in the original stage musical.

Farrar wrote it for the 1978 film.

The stage show had a similar song, All Choked Up, written as a pastiche of Elvis Presley's All Shook Up.

Most 21st-century stage productions use You're the One That I Want in its place.

How many copies did You're the One That I Want sell?

Wikipedia says the single has sold over 4 million copies in the US and UK alone, with estimates of more than 15 million worldwide.

That makes it one of the best-selling singles in history.

In the UK it sold 2 million copies and ranked fifth among the best-selling singles of all time as of 2018.
