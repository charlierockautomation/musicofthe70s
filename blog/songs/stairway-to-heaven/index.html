Stairway to Heaven: Every Section of the 8:02 Explained

By Charlie · Published October 16, 2026

▶ Jump to the video

Stairway to Heaven runs 8:02, and it never appeared on a US single or on the Billboard Hot 100.

Jimmy Page began the music at a cottage in Wales in 1970, and Robert Plant improvised the first lyrics at Headley Grange.

The band played it in public for the first time in Belfast, eight months before Led Zeppelin IV reached shops.

US FM radio then played it millions of times without a single to promote.

This page times each section, names the studios and the instruments, and follows the song through radio, charts and a six-year court case.

The band's wider catalog is on Led Zeppelin songs.

Stairway to Heaven Began in a Welsh Cottage and Took Four Studios

Jimmy Page wrote the music and Robert Plant wrote the lyrics.

The music began in 1970 at Bron-Yr-Aur, a cottage in Wales, after Led Zeppelin's fifth American tour.

Page said the first part came to him there one night, and the idea grew from taped fragments.

Plant's first lyrics came at Headley Grange, partly improvised, and he finished them there during the Led Zeppelin IV sessions in 1971.

Four Places It Was Recorded

Recording began in December 1970 and the song was finished in pieces across several rooms.

  • Basing Street, Island Records' studio in London, where recording began in December 1970.
  • Island Studios, where Page returned to record the guitar solo.
  • The Rolling Stones Mobile Studio at Stargroves.
  • Ronnie Lane's Mobile Studio at Headley Grange, used for the 1971 sessions.

The Instruments Behind the Build

The opening is a layered acoustic arrangement rather than one guitar.

  • Page played a Harmony Sovereign H1260 acoustic and a Fender Electric XII 12-string for the rhythm parts.
  • John Paul Jones overdubbed four recorder tracks, probably soprano, alto, tenor and bass.
  • Jones also played a Hohner Electra-Piano in the middle section.
  • Page's solo came from a 1959 Fender Telecaster that Jeff Beck had given him, through a Supro amp.
  • On stage Page switched to a Gibson EDS-1275 double-neck guitar, and Jones used a Mellotron for the recorder sound.

Stairway to Heaven Without a Single: Radio, Sheet Music and Charts

Atlantic wanted a single, and manager Peter Grant refused in 1972 and 1973.

In 1975 Page told Rolling Stone, "We were careful to never release it as a single."

Wikipedia calls it the most requested song on US FM radio of its time.

Counting the Plays and the Sheet Music

  • US radio sources reported about 2,874,000 plays around the song's 20th anniversary.
  • By 2000 the report was over three million broadcasts.
  • It is the biggest-selling single piece of rock sheet music, averaging about 15,000 copies a year and passing one million in total.
  • Plant gave $10,000 to Portland station KBOO during a pledge drive after a DJ there promised never to play it.

Stairway to Heaven does not appear in this site's Billboard Year-End or weekly Hot 100 data, because it never had a US single to chart.

The Stairway to Heaven Lawsuit and the Backmasking Panic

Two fights followed the song, one in a hearing room and one in federal court.

1982: Playing the Record Backward

In January 1982 a Trinity Broadcasting Network program hosted by Paul Crouch claimed the song hid a message when played in reverse.

In April 1982 a California Assembly committee played it backward at a hearing on warning labels proposed by Assemblyman Phil Wyman.

Swan Song, the band's label, answered, "Our turntables only play in one direction, forwards."

2014 to 2020: The Taurus Case

Mark Andes of Spirit and a trust for Randy California sued in May 2014 over a similarity to the Spirit song Taurus.

The case ran six years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who wrote Stairway to Heaven?

Jimmy Page wrote the music for Stairway to Heaven and Robert Plant wrote the lyrics.

Page began the music in 1970 at Bron-Yr-Aur, a cottage in Wales.

Plant improvised the first lyrics at Headley Grange and finished them during the Led Zeppelin IV sessions in 1971.

How long is Stairway to Heaven and what are its parts?

Stairway to Heaven runs 8:02 and moves through four stages.

It opens with acoustic guitar and recorders, turns to a slow electric middle section, passes through Jimmy Page's guitar solo, and ends in a faster hard rock section.

A vocals-only epilogue closes the track.

Was Stairway to Heaven ever released as a single?

Stairway to Heaven was never a US commercial single.

Manager Peter Grant refused Atlantic's requests for one in 1972 and 1973, and Jimmy Page told Rolling Stone in 1975, "We were careful to never release it as a single."

Radio made it the most requested song on US FM anyway.

When did Led Zeppelin first play Stairway to Heaven live?

Led Zeppelin first played Stairway to Heaven in public at the Ulster Hall in Belfast on 5 March 1971.

That was eight months before the album came out.

The band performed it for the last time as Led Zeppelin in Berlin on 7 July 1980.

Did Led Zeppelin win the Stairway to Heaven lawsuit?

Led Zeppelin won the Stairway to Heaven copyright case over the song Taurus.

A jury found no infringement in 2016, and the full Ninth Circuit ruled for the band on 9 March 2020.

The US Supreme Court declined to hear the case on 5 October 2020, which ended it.
