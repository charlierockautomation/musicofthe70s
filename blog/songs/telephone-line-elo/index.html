Telephone Line ELO: How Jeff Lynne Made the Ring Tone

By Charlie · Published October 21, 2026

▶ Jump to the video

Telephone Line ELO is the 1977 single in which a man waits for a girl to answer a ringing phone.

Jeff Lynne wrote and produced it, and it comes from the album A New World Record.

It reached No. 7 on the Billboard Hot 100 and No. 1 in Canada and New Zealand.

This page covers the ring tone, the recording, the charts, the reviews and the covers.

Telephone Line ELO: The Song Behind the Ring Tone

Jeff Lynne wrote Telephone Line for the 1976 album A New World Record.

Wikipedia calls it a ballad.

A Ring Tone Made for America

Electric Light Orchestra had found success in America.

Lynne wanted an American ring tone for the song.

The band phoned a US number they expected nobody to answer.

They then recreated the ringing on a Moog.

They tuned the oscillators to the same notes they had heard on the line.

What the Lyrics Describe

A man listens to a telephone ringing.

He hopes a girl will answer.

While he waits, he imagines what he would say to her.

The whole song hangs on one phone and one long wait.

Five Genre Tags, One Ballad

Wikipedia files the Telephone Line ELO single under five labels at once.

  • Symphonic rock.
  • Orchestral pop.

Recording and Release

Wikipedia dates the A New World Record sessions to July 1976 at Musicland Studios in Munich, West Germany.

String and choral overdubs were added at De Lane Lea Studios in Wembley.

Wikipedia gives no separate session dates for the Telephone Line ELO track.

The album reached US shops on October 15, 1976, and UK shops on November 19, 1976.

On the Charts: A Top Ten Hit in the US

The Telephone Line ELO single reached No. 7 on the Billboard Hot 100.

Wikipedia says it spent 23 weeks on that chart.

That is about a month longer than any other ELO song managed there.

It is not among the Hot 100 number ones in this site's weekly data.

Reception, Rankings and Screen Appearances

Critics have been kind to Telephone Line ELO ever since.

For the wider story of the band's decade, start at Music of the 70s and follow the rock tags.

What Critics Said

  • Donald Guarisco of AllMusic called the arrangement a "miniature symphony."
  • Bruce Eder of AllMusic said it "might be the best Lennon-McCartney collaboration that never was."
  • Billboard said the telephone effects and the "doo-wah chorus" gave it a "50s feel."
  • Cash Box praised Lynne's vocal and the "unearthy strings."
  • Record World called it a "rock ballad of lost love."

Later Rankings

  • Ryan Reed of Stereogum ranked it ELO's best song.
  • Michael Gallucci of Ultimate Classic Rock ranked it the fourth-best ELO song.
  • Brian Kachejian of Classic Rock History ranked it third.
  • Jeff Lynne listed it among his nine favourite ELO songs in 2022.

Source: Wikipedia article on the song, reception section.

Covers and Later Versions

Lynne has revisited Telephone Line ELO himself, and other artists have recorded it too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who wrote Telephone Line by ELO?

Jeff Lynne wrote and produced Telephone Line for Electric Light Orchestra.

It appears on the 1976 album A New World Record.

The single followed in May 1977 on Jet Records and United Artists Records.

The album was recorded at Musicland Studios in Munich, West Germany.

What is Telephone Line by ELO about?

Telephone Line is about a man who listens to a telephone ringing and hopes a girl will answer.

While he waits, he imagines what he would say to her.

Wikipedia calls the song a ballad.

Billboard heard a 1950s feel in its telephone effects and doo-wah chorus.

How did ELO make the phone ring sound in Telephone Line?

Jeff Lynne wanted an American ring tone because ELO had found success in the US.

The band phoned a US number they expected nobody to answer.

They then recreated the ringing on a Moog by tuning its oscillators to the same notes.

Did Telephone Line reach number one?

Telephone Line reached No. 7 on the Billboard Hot 100 and spent 23 weeks on that chart.

It reached number one in Canada on the RPM chart and in New Zealand.

In the UK it peaked at No. 8.

It was not a Hot 100 number one.

What album is Telephone Line on?

Telephone Line is track two on side one of A New World Record, the 1976 Electric Light Orchestra album.

Jeff Lynne produced the album.

Lynne also re-recorded the song in 2012 for the compilation Mr. Blue Sky: The Very Best of Electric Light Orchestra, released under the ELO name.
