70s Rock Bands: 56 Chart Bands Ranked, With Songs to Play

By Charlie · Published November 9, 2026 · Updated November 9, 2026

Wings had the most Billboard year-end Hot 100 hits of any 70s rock band, 11, ahead of Chicago and Electric Light Orchestra at 8 each.

Wings also had the most No. 1 hits of any rock band, 5, for 13 weeks at the top.

This page ranks all 56 rock groups that placed at least one single on a year-end list from 1970 to 1979.

Each band's biggest hit is ready to play, and every rock band page on this site is sorted by style below.

Wings Under Three Credits

Billboard credited the band as Paul McCartney & Wings, Paul McCartney and Wings, and plain Wings.

This ranking merges all three, which is how Wings reach 11 year-end hits.

"Silly Love Songs" spent 5 weeks at No. 1 in 1976, more than any other Wings single.

Hard Rock and Metal

Queen lead the hard rock bands with 5 year-end hits, and "Bohemian Rhapsody" was their biggest, No. 18 for 1976.

Grand Funk Railroad reached No. 1 twice, with "We're an American Band" and "The Loco-Motion."

Bachman-Turner Overdrive are the only other hard rock band here with a No. 1, "You Ain't Seen Nothing Yet."

  • Queen: Two Nights That Defined Their 70s
  • Brian May and Queen: The Guitar Built From a Fireplace

Progressive and Art Rock

Electric Light Orchestra placed 8 year-end hits without a single week at No. 1.

Yes, Jethro Tull and Pink Floyd each reached the year-end list exactly once, all three in the bottom 15 places.

  • Electric Light Orchestra: Electric Light Orchestra's 11 Hits, 5 Top Tens
  • Pink Floyd: 12 Tracks Played, 1 at Number One

More from this style: 70s progressive rock.

Southern Rock

Atlanta Rhythm Section are the top southern rock band here, with 2 year-end hits.

Their best placing was "So in to You," No. 38 for 1977.

The Charlie Daniels Band followed with "The Devil Went Down to Georgia," No. 50 for 1979.

Southern rock shares its roots with country rock, covered on the 70s country rock page.

Glam Rock

Sweet placed 4 year-end hits, more than any other glam band.

The only other glam band in the ranking reached the list once, with "Bang a Gong (Get It On)" at No. 56 for 1972.

More from this style: glam rock in the 70s.

How This Ranking Was Built

Every number on this page about 1970s rock bands comes from this site's Billboard year-end and weekly Hot 100 files.

Who Counts as a Rock Band

  • Groups only, so solo rock singers like Rod Stewart and Peter Frampton are left out.
  • Rock styles only, so soft rock groups like Bread and the Carpenters sit on the 70s soft rock page instead.
  • Credits that name the same band are merged, such as Grand Funk and Grand Funk Railroad.
  • Ties on year-end hits are broken by chart points: 101 minus the year-end rank, added up across every hit.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was Led Zeppelin inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame?

Led Zeppelin were inducted in 1995.

Steven Tyler and Joe Perry of Aerosmith inducted them, two members of a band that sits in the ranking above.

Which 70s rock band won the Grammy for Record of the Year?

The Eagles won Record of the Year for "Hotel California" at the 20th Annual Grammy Awards.

The same night, Fleetwood Mac's Rumours won Album of the Year over Steely Dan's Aja and the Eagles' own Hotel California album.

Was Foreigner nominated for Best New Artist?

Yes, Foreigner was nominated for Best New Artist at the 20th Annual Grammy Awards.

Debby Boone won the award that year.

How high did "Bohemian Rhapsody" reach in the United States?

Wikipedia reports that "Bohemian Rhapsody" peaked at No. 9 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1976.

It climbed to a new peak of No. 2 in 1992 after it appeared in the film Wayne's World.

When was Queen inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame?

Queen were inducted in 2001.

Dave Grohl and Taylor Hawkins of the Foo Fighters inducted the band.
