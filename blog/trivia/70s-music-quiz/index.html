What This 70s Music Quiz Covers

The 50 questions in this 70s music quiz span the full range of what defined the decade.

No single genre dominates the pool.

  • Classic and hard rock: Led Zeppelin, The Who, Pink Floyd, AC/DC
  • Disco: Donna Summer, the Bee Gees, Chic, Gloria Gaynor
  • Soul and funk: Marvin Gaye, Stevie Wonder, the Temptations, Parliament-Funkadelic
  • Punk and new wave: the Sex Pistols, the Ramones, the Clash, Blondie
  • Singer-songwriters and country: Carole King, Joni Mitchell, Dolly Parton, Willie Nelson
  • Progressive rock: Yes, Jethro Tull, Emerson, Lake & Palmer, Genesis

Questions cover landmark albums, chart-topping singles, and band lineups.

A handful dig into real historical turning points too.

One question asks about the year Elvis Presley died.

Another asks about the album that first sold Michael Jackson as a solo star.

That was years before he left Motown behind for good.

A few questions test specific release years.

Like when the Ramones' self-titled debut came out, or when Patti Smith's Horses arrived.

Others ask about real production details.

Like which trio recorded 1973's Tres Hombres down in Texas.

Or who was still fronting Genesis when Selling England by the Pound came out.

A handful test genuinely surprising facts too.

One question asks which Swedish group won the 1974 Eurovision Song Contest.

Its winning single launched that group's entire global career.

Another asks about the death of a beloved singer-songwriter in a 1973 plane-related tragedy, right after some of his biggest hits.

Every question comes with the same real four-option format, whether it's an easy warmup or a genuine stumper.
