# YouTube Compliance: Full Detail

Set up in the 2026-09-20 audit. The site uses YouTube's embedded player and
IFrame Player API, so the YouTube API Services Terms and Developer Policies apply.
Re-read the current wording before relying on anything here:
- https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies
- https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/required-minimum-functionality
- https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies-guide
- https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/branding-guidelines

## The four standing rules (short form lives in CLAUDE.md)
1. Every YouTube embed is visible, unobscured, at least minimum size, and never a
   hidden or background audio player.
2. Playback starts only from a user action.
3. Every ID has embeddable and madeForKids status on record no more than 30 days old.
4. Before AdSense is applied for: every page with a player has substantial
   non-YouTube content, no ad sits on or overlaps a player (check auto-ad formats
   such as anchor and vignette), and the Privacy Policy is first updated to
   disclose third-party ads and cookies.

## Players: visible, unobscured, minimum size
- Required Minimum Functionality: "Embedded players must have a viewport that is at
  least 200px by 200px." Recommended for 16:9: at least 480 x 270.
- Never place overlays, frames or other visual elements in front of any part of a
  player, including its controls. Never obscure it. Bezels are padding/border AROUND
  the frame, never a layer on top.
- Never diminish standard player features: keep `controls: 1`, fullscreen, keyboard
  controls and the YouTube logo (no `modestbranding`, `controls: 0`, `disablekb`, `fs: 0`).
- Background or audio-only play is prohibited (III.I.9): no player that is not
  displayed in the page, tab or screen the user is viewing. No 1x1, opacity 0,
  `display:none`, `visibility:hidden`, off-screen or clipped players. Never write UI copy
  that says "audio", "audio-only", "no video", "hidden" or "background" about playback.
- Blog embeds: `.video-embed` in css/style.css is 16:9, `min-height: 200px`. Keep the
  plain iframe with `referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"`, no autoplay parameter.
- The floating back-to-top button hides itself while it overlaps a player
  (js/main.js, `.back-to-top.covering`). Any new fixed or sticky element must do the same.
- The six tools do not embed YouTube; they link to YouTube search (`youtubeSearchURL`
  in js/main.js). If a tool ever embeds a player, all rules here apply to it.

## Listen Now (/radio/) architecture
- One shared player, `#ytPlayerHost` inside `.radio-screen`, ABOVE the station controls
  and shared by the Stations panel and every Jukebox tile. The bezel (`.radio-bezel`) surrounds it;
  the note, Continue button and controls sit below it. Frame is 16:9, `min-height: 200px`, up to 480 wide.
- js/radio-player.js: `RadioPlayer` (IFrame API singleton, `loadApi()` loads the script,
  the player is created only inside a Play click) and `ScreenWatch` (is more than half the player on
  screen and the tab visible).
- js/radio.js: queue/UI logic. `advance(auto)` holds auto-advance when the player is off screen and
  shows Continue; it resumes when the player is back on screen or the listener presses Continue.
- Volume: `.radio-volume` (mute button + slider) sits BELOW the frame, never over it. `RadioPlayer.setVolume/setMuted`
  work before the player exists and are applied on ready; the choice is kept in localStorage (`radioVolume`, disclosed
  in the Privacy Policy). Blog embeds rely on YouTube's own volume control (controls:1); do not remove it.
- Desktop Jukebox view pins the screen under the site header (`.grid-mode .radio-screen`, sticky)
  so scrolling the grid does not push the player off screen.
- Non-YouTube content: `<!-- STATION-GUIDE -->` block in radio/index.html is generated by
  `python3 scripts/build_radio_content.py` from data/radio/radio-songs.json. Use `rank`
  (Year-End position) only; `billboard_peak` in this catalog just mirrors rank.

## Playback starts only from a user action
- Nothing may start on page load. Do not add `autoplay=1` to embeds. The `?play=<radio_id>`
  deep link only scrolls to and highlights a tile.
- Automatic playback (song ended, error skip) may start only when the tab is visible and more than half
  of the player is visible: "An API Client must not initiate an automatic playback until the player is
  visible and more than half of the player is visible on the page or screen." Route every automatic
  advance through `advance(true)`. Skip, Continue and tile Play are user actions.
- No more than one player per page may autoplay.
- Nothing may gate, charge for, or reward watching.

## Status data: embeddable and madeForKids, at most 30 days old
- Store: data/youtube-status.json (per ID: embeddable, madeForKids, privacyStatus, checked_at).
- API data may be stored for at most 30 calendar days, then refreshed or deleted.
- Check every ID: `YOUTUBE_API_KEY=... python3 scripts/youtube_status.py` (missing or 25+ days old)
- Full re-check: `... python3 scripts/youtube_status.py --refresh`
- Gate, no API call: `python3 scripts/youtube_status.py --report` (exit 1 if any ID is missing, older
  than 30 days, or blocked while embedded on a page). Run before every push.
- `scripts/verify_post.py` runs the same check for a post's embedded IDs and exits 1 on failure.
- Quota: `videos.list` costs 1 unit per request, 50 IDs per request. 1,040 IDs = 21 units (10,000/day default).
  The catalog's `search.list` is far more expensive (100 units per call, about 100 calls/day).
- Radio: js/radio.js drops madeForKids songs from stations and the Jukebox, and shows non-embeddable
  ones as "Video unavailable". Blog embeds with a blocked ID must be fixed by hand.
- Never download, cache or store the video or audio. Never use scraping (yt-dlp is retired:
  III.E.6 and III.I.14). Find new IDs with `scripts/enrich_youtube_ids.py` (Data API), then run the status script.

## API key
- Use only a key from a Google Cloud project dedicated to this site. Never the
  classicrockartists-automation project.
- Environment variable `YOUTUBE_API_KEY` only. Never print it, write it to a file, or commit it.
  (`scripts/.env` is gitignored, but new work uses the environment variable, not that file.)
- Never embed API credentials in public code or pages.

## Legal pages
- Terms of Use: pages/terms-of-use.html, linked from every footer. It must link to
  https://www.youtube.com/t/terms and say using the site means agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
- Privacy Policy: pages/privacy-policy.html must say the site uses YouTube API Services, link to the
  Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), and disclose every cookie or similar
  technology in use (today: Google Analytics `_ga`, `_ga_ZY77Y8DHV1`, YouTube players, Cloudflare Web
  Analytics, localStorage). Update it whenever a new tracker or embed type is added.
- Attribution: never hide YouTube's logo or links inside players. Anything YouTube-derived shown
  outside a player (titles, thumbnails, logos) must follow the branding guidelines. None is shown today.

## Before applying for AdSense (checklist)
1. Every page with a player has substantial non-YouTube content (III.G.1.d: no ads on a page with
   YouTube API data unless other content "offers enough independent value" without it). Today: blog
   posts have 1,100+ words each; /radio/ has the generated station guide (1,000+ words). Watch /radio/
   in particular; keep growing its own content.
2. No ad on or overlapping a player. Turn off or exclude auto-ad formats that float or overlay
   (anchor, vignette, side rails) on player pages, or use manual placements only, and test on mobile.
3. Update the Privacy Policy FIRST to disclose third-party ads, their cookies and how to opt out (the
   existing "Advertising (future)" paragraph is a placeholder). Only then add any ad code.

## Verify after any change (localhost: `cd ~/musicofthe70s.net && python3 -m http.server 8000`)
- /radio/index.html: player visible at 480x270 desktop and at least 200x200 on a 360px-wide phone; not covered.
- Nothing plays on load; the first Play click creates the player; a tile click plays in the same player.
- Scroll the player off screen mid-song: the next song holds and Continue appears.
- Footer shows Terms of Use on every page; `python3 scripts/youtube_status.py --report` passes.
