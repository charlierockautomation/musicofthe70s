Contact Music of the 70s

Music of the 70s is written and maintained by Charlie Gillingham, a content strategist based in Blackfalds, Alberta.

The fastest way to reach me is email: hello@musicofthe70s.net.

Corrections

Chart data and artist facts on this site come from Billboard's Year-End Hot 100 lists and public reference sources. Mistakes happen. If you spot a wrong chart position, a misattributed songwriting credit, or any other factual error, email the page URL and what's wrong, and I'll fix it.

Licensing and permissions

If you'd like to reuse text, data, or design elements from this site, or have a question about how the site's tools and content can be used, email me with the details.

Feedback on the tools

The Random 70s Song Generator, Birthday #1 Finder, Mood Song Matcher, Random Artist Picker, Trivia Quiz, and Decade Wheel are all built and maintained here. If one is behaving oddly, or you have an idea for a tool that's missing, I want to hear about it.

Response time

This is a one-person site, so please allow a few business days for a reply.
