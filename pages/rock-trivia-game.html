A Rock Trivia Game Built on Real Chart and Artist Data

Most online quizzes recycle the same 20 trivia questions about Led Zeppelin and the Rolling Stones. This rock trivia game doesn't. Every question is generated straight from this site's own verified database of 219 rock acts active in the 1970s, covering real album titles, real release years, real songs and real formation dates. There's no guessing and no invented trivia, just facts pulled directly from the source data.

The pool currently holds more than 1,300 questions across four genres, so a single sitting rarely repeats the same round twice. Whether you grew up on arena rock, discovered prog through your parents' record collection, or came in through punk's back door, there's a category built for you.

Four Rock Categories to Choose From

  • Classic Rock — The Rolling Stones, Fleetwood Mac, the Eagles and the FM-radio staples that defined the decade.
  • Hard Rock — AC/DC, Deep Purple, Aerosmith and the bands that turned amplifiers up past ten.
  • Prog Rock — Yes, Genesis, Pink Floyd and the side-long suites built for headphones.
  • Punk & New Wave — The Clash, Blondie, Television and the acts that tore up the rulebook.

Play the Daily Challenge, Build a Streak

Every visitor gets the same 10-question Daily Challenge on a given day, so you can compare scores with friends who play the same round. Complete it once, and it locks until tomorrow, when a fresh set unlocks. Come back day after day and your streak counter keeps climbing, right up until you miss a day and it resets.

How This Rock Trivia Game Works

Pick a category, or jump straight into the Daily Challenge. Each round pulls 10 random questions from that category's pool, with answer order shuffled so no two rounds ever look identical, even on a repeat category. Answer, get instant feedback with a real fact about the artist, and see your final score against your personal best. Share your result, then come back tomorrow for a new Daily Challenge.

Looking for something broader than rock? The 70s Music Trivia Quiz spans every genre of the decade, from disco to soul to country. And if this game sends you down a rabbit hole on a specific band, the Trivia and Artists sections have deep dives worth reading.
